How it works

Three moves. Everything is attributed.

  1. Employee badge 1042
    Door unlocked
    1

    Identify

    The employee presents their badge. The door unlocks.

  2. 2

    Take

    Several items, in any order, like a fridge.

  3. Employee 1042 · 3 × gloves · 14:32
    Employee 0871 · 1 × goggles · 14:37
    Employee 1042 · 2 × wipes · 14:41
    3

    Attribute

    The door closes; the system identifies what left and attributes it to the employee. Inventory updates everywhere.
